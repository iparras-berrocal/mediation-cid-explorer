How to use

Select an ensemble-filtering method and a region. Click a CID sector or label to display anomaly ranges across increasing global warming levels. Hover over the anomaly ranges to inspect their values.

Filtering methods

Method A — Quality-based filtering: excludes simulations rated as Bad.
Method B — Constrained ensemble: includes simulations that reach GWL4 and are not rated as Bad.
Method C — Full ensemble: includes all available simulations.

Displayed information

The radial plot summarizes confidence in projected CID changes and historical trend evidence. The detail panel presents the ensemble mean, P10–P90 range, min–max spread and number of simulations.
